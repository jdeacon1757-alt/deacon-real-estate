About Sagamore

Sagamore is a gated golf community in Noblesville (ZIP 46060) built around The Sagamore Club. The club's private 18-hole Jack Nicklaus Signature course opened in 2003. Homes include custom single-family homes and condominiums known as The Enclave.

  • Golf: a private, par-72 Jack Nicklaus Signature course
  • Amenities: a full-service clubhouse, junior Olympic-size pool, fitness center and tennis
  • Homes: custom single-family homes and the condominiums of The Enclave
  • Schools: School assignment can vary by address, so I'll confirm the exact schools for any home you're considering.
