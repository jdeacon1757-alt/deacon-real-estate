About Chatham Hills

Chatham Hills is a private golf community on Westfield's north side, built on about 800 acres of rolling former farmland with creeks, woods and lakes. It's anchored by The Club at Chatham Hills, which opened in 2016 and is home to the final golf course designed by Pete Dye.

  • Location: north Westfield, along Tomlinson Road (ZIP 46074)
  • Golf: an 18-hole Pete Dye championship course plus a 9-hole executive course. The club has also hosted LIV Golf events.
  • Schools: Westfield Washington Schools

Homes in Chatham Hills

Chatham Hills isn't one kind of house. It's several neighborhoods inside one community, which is why its prices cover such a wide range:

  • Townhomes: The Towns at Chatham, near one of the community's lakes
  • Chatham Village: village-style cottages and single-family homes with front porches
  • Single-family homes along the golf course from builders such as Estridge
  • Lindley Ridge and custom estates: the community's luxury end, where many homes are priced above $1 million

Because each section has its own builders, HOA dues and club access, two homes a few streets apart can be very different purchases. Before you tour, I'll pull current sales for the exact section you're looking at, so you know what homes there actually sell for.

The club and amenities

The Club at Chatham Hills is a private club. Its amenities include the two golf courses, a clubhouse, pools, a fitness center, tennis, dining and trails throughout the community. Which amenities you get, and what they cost, depends on your section and membership level. Some newer sections include a social membership. Membership terms change, so confirm the current details with the club before you buy.

Who Chatham Hills fits

  • Golfers who want a Pete Dye course down the street
  • Buyers who want a resort-style club, pools, fitness and social events built into the neighborhood
  • Families who want Westfield Washington Schools and a newer home
  • Downsizers who want a lower-maintenance townhome or cottage while staying near the club

It's a fit if you want an amenity-rich, master-planned community. If you'd rather have a large lot with no HOA, other parts of Westfield will suit you better. I'll help you compare.

Buying or selling in Chatham Hills

Pricing a home here takes more care than most of Westfield, because builder, section, lot, golf-course views and club access all change the value. FAQ

Common questions

Where is Chatham Hills in Westfield, Indiana?

Chatham Hills is on Westfield's north side near Tomlinson Road, in ZIP code 46074.

Who designed the Chatham Hills golf course?

The 18-hole championship course at The Club at Chatham Hills was designed by Pete Dye and opened in 2016. It was the final course he designed. The club also has a 9-hole executive course.

What school district serves Chatham Hills?

Chatham Hills is in Westfield Washington Schools. Check the district boundary map for the exact elementary school for a given address.

Is golf membership required to live in Chatham Hills?

It depends on the section of the community and the current club policies, and some newer sections include a social membership. Confirm current membership terms with The Club at Chatham Hills before you buy.

What types of homes are in Chatham Hills?

Townhomes, village-style cottages, single-family golf-course homes and custom luxury estates, built by several different builders.
