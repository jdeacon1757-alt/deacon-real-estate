Chatham Hills, Westfield

A private golf community in Westfield with a Pete Dye–designed course and homes from townhomes to custom estates.

Talk With Jacob → Call or Text (574) 780-1143

About Chatham Hills

Chatham Hills is a private golf community on Westfield's north side, set on rolling land with creeks, woods and lakes. It's anchored by The Club at Chatham Hills, whose championship golf course was designed by Pete Dye and opened in 2016.

  • Location: north Westfield, along Tomlinson Road (ZIP 46074)
  • Golf: an 18-hole championship course and a 9-hole executive course, both designed by Pete Dye
  • Schools: Westfield Washington Schools

Homes in Chatham Hills

Chatham Hills isn't one kind of house. It's several neighborhoods inside one community, which is why its prices cover such a wide range:

  • Townhomes: The Towns at Chatham, near one of the community's lakes
  • Chatham Village: village-style cottages and single-family homes with front porches
  • Single-family homes along the golf course from builders such as Estridge
  • Lindley Ridge: custom estate homes

Because each section has its own builders, HOA dues and club access, two homes a few streets apart can be very different purchases. Before you tour, I'll pull current sales for the exact section you're looking at, so you know what homes there actually sell for.

The club and amenities

The Club at Chatham Hills is a private club. Along with the two golf courses, the club offers amenities such as pools, a fitness center and dining. What you can use, and what it costs, depends on your section and membership. Membership terms change, so confirm the current details with the club before you buy.

Who Chatham Hills fits

  • Golfers who want a Pete Dye–designed course in the neighborhood
  • Buyers who want club amenities built into the neighborhood
  • Families who want Westfield Washington Schools and a newer home
  • Downsizers who want a lower-maintenance townhome or cottage while staying near the club

If you'd rather skip club life and an HOA, other parts of Westfield may suit you better. I'll help you compare.
