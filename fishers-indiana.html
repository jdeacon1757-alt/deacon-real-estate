Fishers at a glance

Fishers is a Hamilton County city northeast of Indianapolis, in Delaware and Fall Creek Townships. The 2020 census counted 98,977 residents. It was a small town for most of its history and grew quickly from the 1980s on.

  • County: Hamilton County, alongside Carmel, Noblesville and Westfield
  • Getting around: I-69 connects Fishers with Indianapolis
  • Geist: Fishers annexed the area around Geist Reservoir, which added about 2,200 homes to the city

Schools: Hamilton Southeastern Schools

Fishers is served by Hamilton Southeastern Schools, which has two high schools: Hamilton Southeastern High School and Fishers High School. Your address decides which schools you're zoned for, so check the district's boundary map before you write an offer. I'm happy to do that with you.

Things to do

  • Nickel Plate District: downtown Fishers, with an amphitheater that hosts a weekly farmers market, concerts and festivals.
  • Geist Reservoir: a reservoir on the city's southeast side, built in 1943 by the Indianapolis Water Company.
  • Nickel Plate Trail: a walking and biking trail through the city.
  • Shopping and entertainment: Topgolf and IKEA are in Fishers.
