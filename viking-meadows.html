About Viking Meadows

Viking Meadows is a neighborhood in Westfield (ZIP 46074) along the Monon Trail, near US-31. It includes several sections, such as the Meadowlands at Viking Meadows, the Enclave at Viking Meadows, Blue Grass at Viking Meadows and Two Gaits at Viking Meadows.

  • Schools: Westfield Washington Schools
  • Amenities: a clubhouse, pool, fitness center, indoor basketball, tennis and a playground
  • Trail access: the neighborhood sits along the Monon Trail
  • Homes: single-family homes, including ranches, plus townhomes in some sections
  • HOA: billed quarterly; dues vary by section

Who Viking Meadows fits

  • Buyers who want resort-style neighborhood amenities without a golf club
  • Walkers, runners and cyclists who want the Monon Trail close by
  • Buyers who want quick access to US-31
