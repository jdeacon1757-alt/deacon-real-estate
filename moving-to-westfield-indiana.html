Westfield at a glance

Westfield is a Hamilton County city just north of Carmel, and it's one of the fastest-growing cities in Indiana. A 2025 special census by the U.S. Census Bureau counted 64,407 residents, up from 46,410 in 2020. For buyers, that growth means plenty of newer homes and new-construction communities. It also means lots of road work.

  • County: Hamilton County, alongside Carmel, Fishers and Noblesville
  • Main ZIP code: 46074 (a small part of the city uses 46062)
  • Getting around: US-31 and State Road 32 run through the city, and the Monon Trail runs north–south through it
  • Schools: Westfield Washington Schools

Schools: Westfield Washington Schools

For a lot of families, schools are the reason they look at Westfield in the first place. The whole city is served by one district, Westfield Washington Schools, which has a simple path through the grades:

  • Grades K–4: six elementary schools: Carey Ridge, Maple Glen, Monon Trail, Oak Trace, Shamrock Springs and Washington Woods
  • Grades 5–6: Westfield Intermediate School
  • Grades 7–8: Westfield Middle School
  • Grades 9–12: Westfield High School

Because each grade band has one building, your elementary school depends on your address, but every family ends up at the same intermediate, middle and high school. If a specific elementary school matters to you, check the district's boundary map before you write an offer. I'm happy to do that with you.

Things to do

  • Grand Park Sports Campus: the city's 400-acre sports campus, which drew more than 5 million visits in 2023.
  • Grand Junction Plaza: the downtown park on Union Street, with a stage, a playground and community events like the Westfield International Festival.
  • The Monon Trail: walk or bike south toward Carmel and Indianapolis.
  • Golf: the private Club at Chatham Hills, with a Pete Dye–designed course.

Neighborhoods and housing

Westfield has a lot of new construction, from townhomes to golf-course estates. A few of the neighborhoods buyers ask about most:

  • Chatham Hills: a private golf community on the north side with a Pete Dye course, townhomes, cottages and custom estates
  • Bridgewater Club, Andover, Viking Meadows and Springmill

Builders active in Westfield include Estridge, Drees, Lennar, David Weekley, M/I, Fischer, Beazer and Olthof. See my Westfield new construction guide. A new build can be a great move, but builder contracts are written to protect the builder. Bring your own agent to the first visit. Ask the builder about its agent policy before your first visit, since some require your agent to be with you from the start.

The honest trade-offs

  • Construction: fast growth means road projects and new subdivisions going up. Before you buy, check what's planned near the house you like.
  • Location within the city matters: Westfield covers a lot of ground. The south side is closer to Carmel and Indianapolis, and the far north side is more rural.

FAQ

Common questions

What ZIP code is Westfield, Indiana?

Most of Westfield is in ZIP code 46074. Some homes on the south side of the city use 46062.

What school district is Westfield in?

Westfield is served by Westfield Washington Schools: six elementary schools for grades K–4, then Westfield Intermediate (5–6), Westfield Middle (7–8) and Westfield High School (9–12).

How big is Westfield, Indiana?

A 2025 special census by the U.S. Census Bureau counted 64,407 residents, up from 46,410 in the 2020 census.

Is Westfield a good place to buy a home?

Westfield draws buyers for its schools, new construction, Grand Park and its location north of Carmel and Indianapolis. The trade-offs are ongoing construction and longer drives from the far north side. Call or text Jacob at (574) 780-1143 to talk through whether it fits you.

Do I need my own agent to buy new construction in Westfield?

It's strongly recommended. The builder's sales staff work for the builder, while your own agent reviews the contract, upgrades and timeline for you. Ask the builder about its agent policy before your first visit.
