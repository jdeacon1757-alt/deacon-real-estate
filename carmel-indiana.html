Carmel at a glance

Carmel is the Hamilton County city just north of Indianapolis, between the White River on the east and the Boone County line on the west. The 2020 census counted 99,757 residents, and the city covers about 49 square miles in Clay Township.

  • County: Hamilton County, alongside Westfield, Fishers and Noblesville
  • ZIP codes: 46032 and 46033 (some homes with a Carmel mailing address, such as parts of Bridgewater Club, are in Westfield Washington Schools)
  • Getting around: US-31 (Meridian Street) and Keystone Parkway run north–south, and the Monon Trail runs through the middle of the city
  • Roundabouts: Carmel built 155 roundabouts between 1997 and 2025, so you'll use them a lot

Schools: Carmel Clay Schools

Carmel Clay Schools serves almost all of Carmel:

  • Grades K–5: 11 elementary schools
  • Grades 6–8: three middle schools: Carmel Middle, Clay Middle and Creekside Middle
  • Grades 9–12: one high school, Carmel High School

The district enrolled about 16,000 students in the 2024–25 school year. Your address decides your elementary and middle school, so check the district's boundary map for any home before you write an offer. I'm happy to do that with you.

Things to do

  • Carmel Arts & Design District: shops and restaurants along Main Street and Range Line Road in the older part of town.
  • Carmel City Center and the Palladium: the Palladium is the concert hall at the Center for the Performing Arts, next to City Center's shops and restaurants.
  • The Monon Trail: walk or bike through Carmel north toward Westfield or south toward Indianapolis.

FAQ

Common questions

How many people live in Carmel, Indiana?

The 2020 census counted 99,757 residents in Carmel.

What school district serves Carmel?

Carmel Clay Schools serves almost all of Carmel, with 11 elementary schools (K–5), three middle schools (6–8) and Carmel High School (9–12).

Why does Carmel have so many roundabouts?

The city replaced many intersections with roundabouts to improve traffic flow. Carmel built 155 roundabouts between 1997 and 2025.

What ZIP codes are in Carmel?

Carmel uses ZIP codes 46032 and 46033. Some homes with a Carmel mailing address are actually in Westfield Washington Schools, so check the school district on every listing.
