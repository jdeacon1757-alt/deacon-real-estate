Holliday Farms

A 600-acre golf community in Zionsville with two Pete Dye–designed courses, from the developer of Chatham Hills and Bridgewater.

About Holliday Farms

Holliday Farms is a 600-acre golf community in Zionsville (ZIP 46077), on the west side of Michigan Road north of Willow Road. Henke Development Group, which also built Bridgewater Club and Chatham Hills, developed it on former Holliday family farmland. Pete and Alice Dye planned the course decades earlier, and course construction began in 2019.

  • Schools: Zionsville Community Schools
  • Golf: an 18-hole championship course and a 9-hole executive par-3 course, both Pete Dye designs
  • Club: The Club at Holliday Farms, with dining, fitness, swimming and tennis
  • Homes: luxury custom homes from select builders

Who Holliday Farms fits

  • Golfers who want a private Pete Dye course at home
  • Luxury buyers who want Zionsville schools
  • Buyers who like Chatham Hills but want to be on the Zionsville side
