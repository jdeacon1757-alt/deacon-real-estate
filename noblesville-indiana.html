Noblesville at a glance

Noblesville is the county seat of Hamilton County and the oldest of its four largest cities. The 2020 census counted 69,604 residents, making it Indiana's 10th most populous city.

  • County: Hamilton County, alongside Carmel, Fishers and Westfield
  • Downtown: a traditional Indiana courthouse square, built around the Hamilton County Courthouse (completed in 1879)
  • Water: the White River runs through the city, and Morse Reservoir is on the north side

Schools

Most of Noblesville is served by Noblesville Schools, which includes Noblesville East and West Middle Schools and Noblesville High School. Parts of Noblesville are served by Hamilton Southeastern Schools, the Fishers-based district.

Because two districts cover Noblesville, always check the school district on a listing. I'll confirm it for any home you're considering.

Things to do

  • The courthouse square: local shops and restaurants around the historic Hamilton County Courthouse.
  • Morse Reservoir: a man-made reservoir completed in 1956, used for boating and other water sports.
  • The White River: popular for kayaking and canoeing in the summer.
  • Ruoff Music Center: the outdoor concert venue in Noblesville.
