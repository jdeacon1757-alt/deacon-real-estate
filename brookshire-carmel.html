Brookshire, Carmel

An established east-Carmel neighborhood built around Brookshire Golf Club, the City of Carmel's public golf course.

Talk With Jacob → Call or Text (574) 780-1143

About Brookshire

Brookshire is an established neighborhood in east Carmel (ZIP 46033). It stretches from 116th Street to 126th Street off Gray Road and is built around Brookshire Golf Club. The 18-hole course was designed by William Diddel and played its first round in 1971. The City of Carmel bought the course in 2007 to keep it a public amenity.

  • Schools: Carmel Clay Schools
  • Golf: Brookshire Golf Club, an 18-hole, par-72 public course owned by the City of Carmel
  • Clubhouse: a newer clubhouse at the course
  • Homes: established single-family homes with mature trees, many along the course

Who Brookshire fits

  • Golfers who want a course in the neighborhood without private club dues
  • Buyers who want an established neighborhood with mature trees
  • Families who want Carmel Clay Schools on the east side
