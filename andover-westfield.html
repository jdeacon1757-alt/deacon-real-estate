About Andover

Andover is a large neighborhood on the south side of Westfield, with addresses in ZIP code 46062. It's made up of several sections, including Andover North, the Enclave at Andover and the Landings at Andover.

  • Schools: Westfield Washington Schools
  • Amenities: walking and biking trails, a playground and a dog park
  • HOA: billed annually; dues vary by section
  • Homes: mostly single-family homes, many built from the late 2000s on

Who Andover fits

  • Buyers who want a newer home and neighborhood trails without a club membership
  • Families who want Westfield Washington Schools on the south side of the city, closer to Carmel
  • Move-up buyers comparing sections with different home sizes and price points
