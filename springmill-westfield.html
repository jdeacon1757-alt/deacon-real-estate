About the Springmill area

"Springmill" covers several neighborhoods along Spring Mill Road on Westfield's west side, in ZIP code 46074. Names to know include Springmill Villages, Meadows at Springmill Villages, Springmill Park, and Lennar's Springmill Towns and Springmill Villas, located on Spring Mill Road just south of State Road 32.

The name also covers a separate Springmill area across the line in Carmel (46032), so check the city and school district on any listing.

  • Schools (Westfield side): Westfield Washington Schools
  • Homes: single-family homes, plus low-maintenance three-story townhomes (Springmill Towns) and paired villas (Springmill Villas) from Lennar

Who Springmill fits

  • Buyers who want a newer home on Westfield's west side
  • Buyers who want a low-maintenance townhome or villa in Westfield Washington Schools

Both. Several Springmill neighborhoods are in Westfield (46074) along Spring Mill Road, and there's a separate Springmill area in Carmel (46032). Check the city and school district on each listing.

What schools serve Springmill in Westfield?

Westfield-side Springmill neighborhoods are in Westfield Washington Schools.
