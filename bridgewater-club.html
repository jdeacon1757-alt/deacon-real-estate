Bridgewater Club

An established private golf club community on Westfield's east side, in Westfield Washington Schools.

About Bridgewater Club

Bridgewater Club is a private golf club community on the east side of Westfield, along East 161st Street. It was developed by Henke Development Group starting in 2002, the same developer behind Chatham Hills.

  • Schools: Westfield Washington Schools
  • Mailing addresses: many homes have a Carmel (46033) mailing address even though they're in Westfield Washington Schools, so don't rule out a listing because it says "Carmel"
  • Homes: single-family homes and townhomes

The club

The Bridgewater Club's 18-hole championship course plays 7,098 yards from the back tees at par 72. The clubhouse is about 90,000 square feet, with dining, an indoor/outdoor pool, a fitness center and indoor golf facilities.

What's included with a home, such as club access and which amenities, varies by home and section. Check the HOA and club terms for the specific home before you buy, and I'll help you get them.

Who Bridgewater Club fits
