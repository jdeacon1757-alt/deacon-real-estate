How I sell your home

Selling is a project with a lot of moving parts. My job is to price it right, put it in front of the right buyers and agents, and keep you informed until the day you close.

  • Pricing from real comps: I compare your home with recent sales of the same style of home, not just the same ZIP code, so the price is grounded in what buyers actually pay.
  • A real launch day: I build a "Just Listed" flyer and email it directly to hundreds of local agents the day you list, so the people who work with buyers hear about your home right away.
  • You hear what we hear: I follow up with agents after showings for their feedback and pass it along, so we can adjust if we need to.
  • Negotiation and closing: I handle offers, counters, inspection requests and the timeline through closing, with my transaction coordinator keeping the paperwork on track.

FAQ

Common questions

How do you decide on a list price?

I compare your home with recent sales of similar homes nearby, matching the style of home, then adjust for size, condition and updates. You'll see the comps I used.

How do you market a new listing?

On launch day I send a Just Listed flyer by email to hundreds of local agents, alongside the MLS listing, so agents with active buyers hear about your home right away.

Do you work with sellers who also need to buy?

Yes. I help clients plan the timing of selling and buying, and I can connect you with lenders who offer options for buying before you sell.

What areas do you cover?

Westfield, Carmel, Noblesville, Fishers, Zionsville and the rest of Hamilton County, plus the north side of Indianapolis.
