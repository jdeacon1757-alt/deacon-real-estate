My Story

I learned the hard way, so you don't have to.

21

The age I bought my first investment property and learned what a bad transaction costs.

I didn't get into real estate because it was easy or flashy. I got into it because I learned early how important the right guidance really is.

At 21, I purchased my first investment property. The transaction didn't go as planned. Unexpected issues surfaced, details were missed, and I quickly realized how costly it can be when you don't fully understand what's happening behind the scenes. That experience shaped how I approach real estate today.

It pushed me to build a business centered on trust, clear communication and education, so clients always know where they stand and why decisions are being made. Real estate should feel straightforward, not confusing or rushed.

Today, I work with buyers, sellers and investors throughout central Indiana, helping them make confident, informed decisions backed by real numbers and honest expectations. I don't just focus on getting deals done. I focus on representing people the right way, protecting their interests and making sure there are no surprises along the way.
